# Pocket Mech Arena

Twin-stick, top-down mech shooter made in Godot 4.7 (GL Compatibility). Plays in landscape or portrait.

**Play in your browser:** https://senseinatezz.github.io/pocket-mech-arena-overdrive/
**Windows download:** see [Releases](https://github.com/SenseiNatezz/pocket-mech-arena-overdrive/releases)

## How to play
Eight levels on painted top-down maps: Frozen Outpost, Jungle Megafactory, Alien Outpost, Skyway
Junction, Ruined Plaza, the Volcanic Forge, the Titan Graveyard and the Orbital Dry Dock. Survive 5 waves
of robots on each, then a boss drops in (Warden, Ronin, Seraph, Warden Mk-II, Shogun, VULCAN the Forge
Titan, SCARAB the Dune Colossus and ZENITH the Orbital Dreadnought). Every boss has three phases and an
OVERDRIVE at 20% health (aura, faster attacks, nova rings). Overdrive builds up from the damage
you deal. Die during a boss fight and RETRY BOSS puts you straight back into it.

**Save Backup** (Settings): copy your whole save as a text code and load it on another device or browser.
**Add to Home Screen:** the web version installs as a full-screen app with its own icon.

**New Game:** pick a difficulty (Easy / Normal / Hard / Extreme: more, tougher, faster enemies and
beefier bosses), then your starting weapon on the Loadout screen.

**Enemies:** Scrap Hound, Scrap Gunner, Blade Striker (energy-katana dash), Aegis Guardian (frontal
shield: flank it), Lancer (laser-locked rail sniper) and the armored Heavy Tank. The Volcanic Forge
adds its own: the Magma Crawler (drips lava, lunges, bursts into a lava pool), the Anvil (hammer
leap-slam) and the Slagspitter (lobs molten globs; its furnace tank is weak from behind). The Titan
Graveyard brings the Dune Burrower (tunnels under the sand and erupts beneath you), the Sand Skiff
(circles and rams) and the Heliostat (sun-lance sniper); the Orbital Dry Dock brings the Void Skimmer
(blinks beside you), the Mag Mine Drone (seeds the deck with mines) and the Grav Tug (tractor beam).

**Leveling up:** defeated enemies drop XP shards. Each level-up pauses the game and offers 3 upgrades
(4 with the VX-7 Standard) from 21: Target Lock, Triple Shot, Defense Shield, Piercing Rounds, Rapid
Fire, Power Core, Armor Plating, Thrusters, Orbit Blades, Explosive Rounds, Salvage Repair,
Scavenger, Critical Targeting, Bit Drones, Micro-Missile Pod, Arc Emitter, Impulse Burst, Reactive
Armor, Cryo Rounds, Redline and Reactor Overcharge (a 22nd, Salvage Protocol: +15%
scrap per level, joins once the Jackpot contract is done). Two upgrades you've invested in
unlock a gold COMBO card (9 combos, e.g. Cluster Missiles, Storm Bits, Shatter, Executioner). Under the
cards: REROLL (2 per run, +1 per NEXT ARENA), BANISH (1 per run: that upgrade never shows up again) and
SKIP (+20 HP instead); the Hangar sells more rerolls and banishes. Max level 20; leveling slows down
after level 5, 10 and 15. Upgrades carry over when you press NEXT ARENA.

**Hangar chassis (8):** NIMBUS-07 (standard; 4 upgrade cards per level-up), GRIFFIN (striker: fast; boosting
slices robots and kills recharge the boost), TUSKER (bulwark: heavy; boost rams robots, and SIEGE MODE braces
it when you stand still: +30% damage, -30% damage taken), TEMPEST (artillery: SALVO rockets), FROSTBLADE
(duelist: IAIDO melee), SOLSTICE (guardian: BARRIER shield blast), AURORA (carrier: starts with 2 Bit Drones,
PETAL SWARM homing blades on kills) and RAVEN CLAW (phantom: PHASE BLINK, always flies).

**Walking mechs:** every mech is two layers - the legs step the way you move (backwards when that is behind
your aim) and the upper body swings round to your aim on top, with its own walk cycle, recoil kick and a slide
on boost. **WALK / FLY:** press V (D-pad up, or the FLY / WALK touch button) to lift off and fly on your
thrusters with the legs tucked, or set down and walk again; the game remembers your pick. **FLIGHT FUEL:**
flying is 35% faster but burns fuel (about 6 s a tank; the arc on the left of your ring and the FLY button
show it). Run dry and you drop and must wait for a full tank; land early and it refills at once. A beep warns
you when the tank is low, the jets sputter out when it runs dry, and a chime says you can fly again. The
Reserve Tank and Jump-Jet Legs parts stretch it. RAVEN CLAW always flies (no fuel): a slower glide and
-20% HP, but +20% damage, and it glides over lava, steam vents and shock panels.

**Living maps:** mechs and robots cast soft shadows (yours drops away when you fly); shots, blasts, the beam
and thrusters light the floor; blasts leave scorch marks, robots leave scrap and walking leaves footprints in
snow and sand; the floor has drawn details (snow drifts, cracks, sand ripples, cables...); the lamps, lava,
crystals and beacons painted on each map pulse, flicker and blink; and each map has its own colour mood.

**Three sectors per level:** a Campaign level is a run over three maps - two lead-in sectors on other maps (picked at random each time), then the level's own map, where its boss waits. Each sector has 8 waves built from the level's own robots, with packs of **Scrap Mites** (small, fast swarm robots) between the tough ones. Your level, upgrades, HP and repair kits carry over, with a patch-up between sectors.

**Rival pilots:** each Campaign arena sends a rival pilot in one of the Hangar mechs in the middle sector
(Frozen Outpost FROSTBLADE, Jungle Megafactory TUSKER, Alien Outpost AURORA, Skyway Junction GRIFFIN, Ruined
Plaza TEMPEST, Volcanic Forge SOLSTICE, Titan Graveyard RAVEN CLAW, Orbital Dry Dock NIMBUS-07), and Endless
sends a random one every 6 waves. A rival keeps its range and circles you, fires 3-shot bursts, boost-dodges
when you hit it, and uses its mech's own move: FAN BURST, AFTERBURNER dash, SIEGE MODE shells, SALVO rockets,
IAIDO frost cut, SUN BARRIER, PETAL SWARM, or BLINK behind you. The wave waits until it's down. Beating one pays
a scrap bounty (bigger the first time) and makes that mech 25% cheaper in the Hangar. Spar with them in the
Weapon Range (RIVAL PILOT button; no rewards there).

**Arena music:** every Campaign arena has its own battle track (it muffles during lulls and opens up as
the fight builds); Endless keeps the shared three-layer battle music.

**Gold Salvager:** every 2-3 minutes of waves a gold drone pops up near you and flees for 11 seconds.
Shoot it down for a random prize: SCRAP FRENZY (all scrap x7 for 20 s), LUCKY (15% of the run's scrap at
once), XP SURGE, a full Overdrive, or rarely a JACKPOT of little gold drones that each pay scrap. The
Hangar's Salvager Beacon (opened by the Gold Rush contract) makes them show up more often.

**Threat tiers:** win a run on Extreme (beat an arena's boss, or reach Endless wave 10) to open THREAT 1,
then each tier opens the next, up to THREAT 10. Pick the tier on the Loadout screen. Every tier adds one
condition on top of the ones before (Armored, Elite Tide, Heavy Ordnance, Bombardment, Boss Escort, Scarce
Repairs, Overclocked, Fortified, Elite Legion, Apex) and +25% scrap, and the first win at each tier with
each weapon pays a one-time bounty (1,000 scrap x the tier). Weapon cards show their best tier.

**Armory Mastery Pool (Armory page):** every bit of weapon mastery XP your runs earn (mastered weapons
too) also fills one shared pool, plus a bonus for every mastery level any weapon gains, so trying new
weapons fills it fastest. Its checkpoints switch on for good: 10% +5% scrap, 25% +1 level-up reroll every
run, 50% +10% XP, 95% FINISHER on every weapon.

**Contracts (Hangar > CONTRACTS):** 40 goals in 5 rows (Rookie, Pilot, Veteran, Ace, Legend) that finish
on their own as you play (a toast mid-run, a list on the results screen). Claim each one's scrap in the
Hangar; claiming a whole row switches on its permanent bonus (+5% scrap, +5% damage, +1 reroll a run, +5%
XP, +10% scrap). Gold Rush opens the Salvager Beacon Hangar row and Jackpot the Salvage Protocol card.

**Mech parts (Hangar > PARTS):** build your mech from four slots: HEAD (sensors), ARMS (weapon handling),
LEGS (movement) and BACKPACK (booster). Each starts on a free STOCK part; the 14 others cost scrap, and
every one trades a strength for a weakness, most with a perk on top. Head: Longsight Optics (+crit),
Salvage Dish (+pickup radius and scrap), Lock-On Head (starts runs with Target Lock). Arms: Gunner (+gun
fire rate, STEADY: no recoil), Brawler (+melee speed, SCRAPPER: melee kills repair), Reactor (+overdrive gain
and damage, slower attacks). Legs: Sprinter (+speed and dash), Anchor (+HP), Hover Skirt (HOVER: lava
pools, steam vents and shock panels can't hurt you), Jump-Jet Legs (flight fuel refills 60% faster).
Backpack: Twin Booster (DOUBLE DASH), Missile Rack (starts runs with the Micro-Missile Pod), Overburn Booster
(starts runs with Impulse Burst), Reserve Tank (60% more flight fuel, slower to refill); each backpack
has its own thruster flame. The cards show what a part changes against the one you have on. Buying a part
puts it on, and you can swap between owned parts any time. The Hover Skirt, Twin Booster and Missile Rack
open with the Campaigner, Survivor and Tank Buster contracts.

**Settings:** difficulty, screen shake, volumes, radio voice, camera zoom (Far / Normal / Close), Text Size
(Small / Normal / Large: scales the menus, HUD and touch buttons; the battlefield view stays the same),
Dynamic Camera (the view pulls out as robots crowd in and during boss fights, punches in on big kills,
glides over to a boss as it lands), Damage Numbers, controller Rumble, fullscreen (also F11 / Alt+Enter), CONTROLS (rebind every keyboard / mouse action), and on touch
screens Auto-Fire (fires at the auto-aim target by itself) and the touch layout: Simple (FIRE, BOOST,
ALT FIRE and BEAM; Repair and Overdrive trigger on their own) or Full.

**Weapons (Armory):** 13 weapons, unlocked by the total number of waves you've beaten in any mode:
Beam Rifle (start), Beam Saber (3), Sniper Beam (5), Dual Beam Pistols (6: one in each fist firing in turn,
very fast but light hits, short range), Dual Beam Sabers (8), Beam Katana (10),
Gatling Cannon (13), Beam Spear (15), Rocket Launcher (18), Energy Axe (20), Beam Scythe (25) and
Physical Greatsword (30). The Energy Sword unlocks by defeating VULCAN in the Volcanic Forge (or after
40 waves): a 4-hit combo whose finisher, CRESCENT NOVA, spins and launches a piercing crescent wave.
Melee weapons have combos with a finisher on the last hit.

**Weapon Range (main menu):** every weapon unlocked, spawn any enemy or boss, and you can't be
destroyed. Switch weapons with 1-9, 0, -, = / mouse wheel / Tab or the weapon bar.

**Tutorial:** new pilots are offered a 2-minute guided training mission before their first run (also under Settings > Tutorial). It covers moving, attacking, the boost dash, WALK OR FLY (take off, run the fuel tank dry, wait for the refill), scatter shot, Beam Cannon, a real fight, level-ups, repair kits and Overdrive.

**Endless Mode (main menu):** endless waves on a random open map. Each wave is bigger and tougher, a
Warden boss arrives every 5 waves, and your best wave is saved. Six extra robots join the later waves:
Hornet drones, the Coil serpent, the mine-laying Widow, the flamethrower Cinder, the shield-commander
Bastion and the armored Tidebreaker crab (all spawnable in the Weapon Range too).

| Action | Keyboard / Mouse (rebindable) | Gamepad | Touch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Move | WASD / Arrows | Left stick | Left thumb stick (360°) |
| Aim | Mouse | Right stick | Automatic: locks onto the nearest enemy (drag on FIRE to aim by hand) |
| Fire | Left mouse | RT | Automatic with Auto-Fire (or hold the big FIRE button) |
| Scatter shot | Right mouse | LT | ALT FIRE |
| Boost (dash, crosses pits) | Space | LB | BOOST |
| Self-repair | F | B | Automatic below 35% HP (Simple) / REPAIR |
| Overdrive | Shift | R3 | Automatic when full (Simple) / OVERDRIVE |
| Use (repair stations) | R | A | USE |
| Pause | Esc | Start | II |

## Project layout
- `autoload/` - Controls (input merging), Combat (pooled bullets/effects, damage), Sfx, Game (scene flow, endless mode, weapon unlocks, settings), Upgrades (XP, levels, level-up upgrades)
- `player/` - the mech, its camera (aim lead + shake), boost ring, weapons (guns, beam cannon, melee kit)
- `enemies/` - Scrap Hound (chaser), Scrap Gunner (shooter), Blade Striker, Aegis Guardian, Lancer, Heavy Tank (tank); bosses: Warden, `bosses/` Ronin + Seraph
- `environment/` - arena builder, arena scenes, tiles, props (cover, crates, pits, doors...), hazards, encounter zones, parallax skyline
- `ui/` - HUD, menus (title lobby: `main_menu.gd` + `lobby_kit.gd` / `lobby_widgets.gd`; Armory, Hangar, Gun Range panel), touch controls
- `fx/` - shared effects: explosion fireballs, smoke, scorch marks (`blast_fx.gd`, `smoke_puff.gd`), the molten lava shader
- `tools/` - tileset generator, automated tests, autopilot, debug runner, `fx_shots.gd` (weapon / FX close-up captures)
- `art_src/` - source for generated art and sound: `weapons/build_beam_pistol.py` (Blender, the pistol art), `sfx/make_combat_sfx.py` (layered combat sounds)
- `assets/hq/raw/` - source art for the tools only. It has a `.gdignore`, so Godot never imports or exports it. Big painted maps, the Customize turntable and the big sprite sheets (mechs, bosses, props) import as lossy WebP (quality 0.9; small download, works on every platform). Small sprites stay lossless.

Arena size: select an arena scene's root and change `size_in_screens` in the Inspector.

## Tests
Run these from the project folder. `--temp-save` keeps tests away from your real save.
- `godot --headless --path . res://environment/test_range.tscn -- --smoke-test --temp-save` checks controls
- `godot --headless --path . res://environment/test_range.tscn -- --upgrade-test --temp-save` checks upgrades and weapons
- `godot --headless --path . -- --endless --endless-test --temp-save --no-intro` checks the Endless flow
- `godot --headless --path . -- --tutorial --tutorial-test --temp-save` plays through the tutorial
- `godot --headless --path . -- --arena=1 --arena-test --temp-save --no-intro` checks the arena flow
- `godot --headless --path . -- --gun-range --touch --attach=res://tools/touch_test.gd --temp-save` checks the touch controls (auto-aim, FIRE button, move stick)
- `godot --headless --path . -- --arena=6 --no-intro --attach=res://tools/forge_test.gd --temp-save` checks the Volcanic Forge robots and the VULCAN boss
- `godot --headless --path . -- --arena=7 --no-intro --attach=res://tools/frontier_test.gd --temp-save` checks the Titan Graveyard robots and SCARAB (`--arena=8`: the Orbital Dry Dock robots and ZENITH)
- `godot --headless --path . -- --arena=1 --no-intro --attach=res://tools/overdrive_test.gd --temp-save` checks boss phases, OVERDRIVE and the hit-direction markers
- `godot --headless --path . -- --temp-save --attach=res://tools/save_backup_test.gd` checks the save backup code
- `godot --headless --path . -- --gun-range --attach=res://tools/energy_sword_test.gd --temp-save` checks the Energy Sword (unlock, held art, combo + CRESCENT NOVA)
- `godot --headless --path . -- --gun-range --attach=res://tools/chassis_test.gd --temp-save` flies all 8 Hangar chassis and checks each one's stats, art and trick (SALVO, IAIDO, BARRIER, PETAL SWARM, PHASE BLINK)
- `godot --path . --resolution 1280x720 -- --gun-range --temp-save --attach=res://tools/walk_shots.gd --walk-out=DIR` walks a mech through a fixed routine, checks its walking legs and saves a crop per frame (`--walk-demo` = free walking + circling a target; `--hover` = the old hover art)
- `godot --headless --path . -- --arena=4 --no-intro --temp-save --attach=res://tools/rival_test.gd` checks every rival pilot (art, moving, shooting, signature move, bounty, Hangar discount), the middle-wave arrival and the arena's own music
- `godot --headless --path . -- --arena=1 --sector=1 --no-intro --temp-save --attach=res://tools/sector_test.gd` checks the three-sector run (random lead-in maps, 8 waves with Scrap Mite packs, level / upgrades / HP carried over, boss only on the last map)
- `godot --headless --path . res://environment/test_range.tscn -- --goals-test --temp-save` checks the Gold Salvager, Threat tiers and Contracts
- `godot --headless --path . res://environment/test_range.tscn -- --parts-test --temp-save` checks the mech parts (stock defaults, buying, contract locks, saving, every part's stats and perks, the Hangar PARTS tab; add `--portrait` for the phone layout)
- `godot --headless --path . -- --arena=1 --no-intro --temp-save --difficulty=3 --threat=5 --salvager=1 --goals-arena-test` checks Salvager spawning, the Threat boss escort and bounties in a real arena
- `godot --headless --path . -- --temp-save --scrap=4200 --attach=res://tools/lobby_test.gd --lobby-nav-test` checks keyboard / gamepad navigation on the title lobby (add `--portrait` for the phone layout)
